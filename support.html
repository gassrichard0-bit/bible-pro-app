Need help or found a bug? Email me at Gassfamily2017@gmail.com. I read every message.

Do I need to sign up?

No. There is nothing to sign up for. Everything is on your iPhone.

Does it work offline?

Yes. All translations, Strong's, commentaries and reading plans are built in. Only groups need an internet connection.

Where did my notes from the old version go?

Open the app after updating. For about two weeks it brings your notes, highlights, plans and prayers over from the old version once, automatically.

How do groups work?

Groups use iCloud sharing. Create a group and send the invite link; everyone needs to be signed in to iCloud.

Why don't I see the study agent?

It runs on the device and needs an iPhone with Apple Intelligence turned on. On other iPhones it stays hidden.

Will my notes move to a new iPhone?

Your notes live on this device. They move with you when you set up the new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup.
